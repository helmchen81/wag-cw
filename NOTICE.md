## Callsign datasets

`calls-data.js` and `foreign-calls-data.js` contain amateur-radio training data.
Some callsigns may identify real stations or people; synthetic calls can also
coincidentally match real callsigns. Before redistributing or substantially
reusing these datasets, verify the applicable source, privacy and database
rights for the intended jurisdiction and use.

## No official adjudication

Battle Score, Difficulty and simulated contest behavior are training features.
They are not official DARC contest adjudication.
