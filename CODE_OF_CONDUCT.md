# Community code of conduct

This project welcomes constructive, technically focused collaboration.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally
- give actionable, evidence-based feedback
- respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or security-sensitive data
- keep discussions relevant to the project
- accept technical review and correction in good faith

Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, deliberate disruption and the
publication of credentials or personal data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and restrict participation
when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Conduct concerns can be reported privately to abusegame@kabanet.de.
